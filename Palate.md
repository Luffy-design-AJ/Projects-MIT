# Palate

**[▶ Try Palate](YOUR_DEMO_LINK)**

*An Akinator for food discovery.*

## The Idea

What if discovering food felt like playing Akinator? Palate uses an interactive sequence of questions to narrow down dishes based on your flavour preferences.

## How It Works

Inspired by Akinator, Palate uses a Bayesian decision-tree approach to refine its predictions. At each step, a greedy strategy selects the question expected to reduce entropy the most, narrowing the possibilities as efficiently as possible.

The goal is to turn food discovery into an interactive process of exploration rather than another list of recommendations.
